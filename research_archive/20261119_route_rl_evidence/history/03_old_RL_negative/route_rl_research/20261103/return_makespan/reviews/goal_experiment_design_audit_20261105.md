# Independent Experiment-Design Audit: Route RL / Weather / Emergency

Date: 2026-11-05 (Asia/Shanghai)  
Scope: read-only static review of the current G0-G8 evidence, weather/NFZ route-RL records, and P3 closure. No experiment, map generation, solver, training, or production-code change was performed.

## Decision

**The current evidence is not sufficient for a CCF-B-level claim of route-RL superiority, algorithmic novelty, or a completed paper.** It is sufficient to report bounded engineering contracts, several negative/feasibility findings, and a small non-RL calibration result. The current paper-readiness audit likewise records `NOT_READY_FOR_CLAIMED_RL_PAPER` and lists missing G0/G4/G6/G7/G8 evidence (paper-readiness audit, lines 78-114).

The principal blocker is not merely small sample size. There is no completed RL route candidate on the current weather task, no PPO-vs-BC causal comparison, no confirmed strongest same-objective non-RL comparator, and no approved novel physical-route mechanism. P3 failed its registered common-BC free-construction gate before PPO and validation; later exact label fitting on the same stored training states does not change that result (STATE.json, lines 690-777; P3 diagnosis, lines 30-38).

## Verified Facts

1. **Scope and target.** The active action contract is physical `(slot,target)` / `(slot,RETURN)` under fixed deployment, station type, and target-station assignment; operator/subproblem selection is false (STATE.json, lines 57-69). The primary estimand is full-map final-return makespan under per-map epsilon-zero full-cost caps, with 6s and 12s budgets and a 5% practical target; the state explicitly says the target is not achieved (STATE.json, lines 14-28).
2. **G0 is still NO-GO.** The current screens reject the named residual-budget/dual-price, threshold-conditioned, product-credit, event-pair, and generic decoder proposals as distinct novelty. DPTARL already covers direct target/return construction and feasibility filtering; weather/NFZ and hybrid inspection have adjacent prior work. No replacement candidate is registered (G0_REASSESSMENT_20261105.md, lines 6-10 and 24-52).
3. **Existing non-RL evidence is calibration only.** Strict and annealed controls delivered 16/16 rows on four exposed maps, with the same reported mean M_return reduction of 4.620653% at both budgets. These four maps do not establish a powered population effect or strongest-NONRL status (calibration `REPORT.md`, lines 1-14; G0 reassessment, lines 21 and 29).
4. **Previous route-RL evidence is negative/incomplete.** STATE records v2 nine runs/nine dead-end episodes, v3 nine BC+RL runs/72 dead-end episodes, no complete candidate, and no G4 pass (STATE.json, lines 119-128). The exact scope/denominators are preserved in lines 160-191.
5. **P3 is closed.** The fixed 14-map P3 package yielded 5/6 eligible TRAIN and 7/8 eligible VAL anchors, then one 400-epoch BC seed with 1200 updates and zero of five complete free constructions. PPO, continued BC, and all 192 actual VAL calls were zero; the 192 planned rows were skipped (P3 audit, lines 3-11; STATE.json, lines 690-730).
6. **Teacher diagnostics do not rescue P3.** At the first divergence, the checkpoint was wrong on all five saved teacher-prefix states; overall teacher top-1 was 128/138. Epoch 800 later fit all 138 *same stored states*, without saved model, free rollout, VAL, solver, or new map; the P3 closure remains (teacher-state audit, lines 19-53; fit diagnosis, lines 23-38; STATE.json, lines 754-777).
7. **The P3 design itself correctly limited its claims.** It calls itself a bounded feasibility/attribution pilot, not confirmation; at least four eligible VAL maps only permitted descriptive conditional comparisons. It explicitly did not establish a strongest non-RL comparator or G0 novelty (P3 final protocol, lines 3-15 and 39-47).
8. **G5-G8 remain open.** The plan requires map-level inference, a fresh confirmation set after all method/source/budget/statistics are frozen, component ablations, and a clean reproducible package; none is complete (RESEARCH_PLAN_ROUTE_RL.md, lines 88-102).

## Design Gaps and Identifiability Risks

1. **No current experiment identifies an RL effect.** The tiny v2/v3 runs did not yield complete trajectories, and P3 had no PPO or validation call. BC teacher-state accuracy is not a route-level outcome. A future PPO-vs-BC contrast must share the exact BC initialization, demonstrations, architecture, information, action mask, and TRAIN population; PPO must be contrasted with both frozen BC and wall-clock-matched continued BC. Otherwise the result confounds extra optimization/data exposure with RL updates.
2. **Anchor/eligibility conditioning can hide attrition.** Across the accumulated study record, eight anchors are unusable; within fixed P3-14 specifically, TRAIN_2 and VAL_4 failed the weather anchor, leaving 5/6 TRAIN and 7/8 VAL eligible. Reporting M_return only on successful anchors or only successful policy deliveries changes the target population. Report proposal-to-anchor eligibility, anchor failure reasons, and policy delivery for every allocated map; separately label the conditional quality estimand. Do not replace failures or merge different proposal roles into an assumed IID success-rate estimate.
3. **The independent unit is the map, not the seed, episode, state, or method-budget row.** Seeds are nested policy replicates; each map's paired method outputs form a cluster. Current pilot sizes (four calibration maps; P3's at-most-eight VAL proposals) cannot establish the planned population effect. The proposed formal sample count is correctly still open pending power analysis (plan lines 88-90; P3 lines 13-15).
4. **“Strongest non-RL” is not currently established.** Strict/annealed are standard controls and had identical reported M reductions on four calibration maps. Before comparison, select/freeze the strongest same-objective weather-aware route baseline on a disjoint calibration split; include the author anchor, the current strict/annealed procedures, and a route-only ALNS with legal multi-trip repacking (plan lines 69-76). A baseline selected on confirmation outcomes invalidates the contrast.
5. **The registered estimand and pass gates need one canonical version.** Current state/requirements specify a 5% practical mean target at 6s and 12s, while the G4 paragraph in the plan still says 0.3% (STATE lines 14-26; plan lines 78-86). Resolve this in a versioned amendment before any new outcome is observed. Also predefine whether “mean improvement” is the arithmetic mean of per-map relative gains, a ratio of means, or another statistic; these differ.
6. **Two caps and complete-route validity must be enforced per map and method.** The full original cost cap and weather-replayed author cost cap both apply; per-map candidates above either cap are not admissible. The independent replay must check complete return, exact-once service, duty, battery, route legality, and endpoint. Report raw candidate, fallback, and no-delivery distinctly; never score an invalid/late candidate as an improvement (STATE lines 14-28; P3 lines 19-21 and 31).
7. **The operational budget must match the claim.** Per-map 6s/12s comparison should include cold model load, inference, route construction, all candidate generation/restarts/beam, independent validation, serialization, and parent-side delivery parsing. Record training and baseline calibration CPU/wall costs separately and in total; the manuscript must state whether its claim is post-training inference efficiency or total lifecycle resource efficiency. This is required by the plan's cost accounting (lines 81-85), but a new protocol must freeze exact timer boundaries and amortization.
8. **Multiplicity and selection are under-specified for confirmation.** Two budgets, multiple RL seeds/algorithms, BC and continued-BC contrasts, multiple non-RL baselines, and MTC/weather/emergency outcomes create a broad family. The current plan mentions corrected comparison families but does not bind a single primary contrast and correction method. Pre-register one primary route-RL contrast family (6s and 12s jointly required), multiplicity correction, and secondary/exploratory families. Do not select checkpoints, seeds, metric definitions, or subgroups using Formal outcomes.
9. **Novelty is not established by problem combination or simulator detail.** Current direct-route actions, attention, masks, budget conditioning, weather/NFZ inclusion, cache, BC, and endpoint instrumentation are not by themselves a distinct mechanism. A CCF-B algorithm claim needs an exact physical state-to-route-action/update rule, a nearest-prior-art non-equivalence argument, a falsifiable predicted failure/benefit, and G6 ablation against the simplest same-information substitute. The current G0 audit explicitly requires this (G0 reassessment, lines 31-41).
10. **Weather and emergency evidence cannot be pooled as one claim.** The weather route-RL objective is deterministic route construction under weather/NFZ and cost caps; the emergency simulator's engineering contract concerns event decisions, ownership, finite horizon, and delivery accounting. Current emergency evidence is bounded simulator/contract evidence, not real-flight safety or a statistical policy-performance result (paper-readiness audit, lines 8-12, 39-55, 78-85). Any cross-domain claim needs its own frozen estimand, baselines, map/scenario unit, and confirmation design.

## Recommended Next Experiment: A Fresh, Gated Attribution Study

This is a protocol recommendation, **not permission to execute**. P3 identities remain exposed and closed; the follow-on must be newly registered, use fresh map identities, run only on <COMPUTE_HOST>, and have root as the sole experiment launcher.

### Gate 0: Declare the paper claim first

- For a **novel-method paper**, require a new static G0 pass before route-quality work: exact state, physical route action, update/reward, feasibility/termination semantics, nearest-prior-art comparison, and a preregistered mechanism-specific prediction. The learned output must remain physical route decisions only.
- If no mechanism passes G0, explicitly choose an **application/control study** with no novel-RL-mechanism claim. Do not continue calling it a novel algorithm paper or imply that a positive benchmark alone supplies algorithmic novelty.

### Development and Confirmation Allocation

- Freeze an allocation by role before generating identities: fresh TRAIN, fresh DEV/calibration, and a separately held confirmation set opened only after source, model/checkpoint aggregation, comparator, budgets, exclusions, analysis code, and protocol hashes are final. Exclude every historical, calibration, P2, P3, diagnostic, synthetic, and previously exposed map; P3's 14 identities join the permanent exclusion union.
- State the target population precisely as the constraint-reconstructed proposal distribution, including generator code/hash, family/size strata, wind/NFZ distribution, rejection rules, proposal ordinals, and source provenance. Do not call it the author's generator or IID-uniform unless established.
- Use DEV-only anchor feasibility and paired-effect variance to calculate the confirmation sample size. Freeze a power target (recommend 90% power for the 5% effect if resources allow; 80% is a defensible lower bound), alpha, expected attrition, and minimum map count before Formal release. If no defensible variance estimate exists, collect additional DEV maps within the existing envelope rather than treating four calibration maps as a power estimate.
- Every allocated proposal remains in the ledger. Report anchor eligibility rate and reasons over all proposals; require the powered number of eligible confirmation maps before opening Formal. No replacement maps after author/weather/route failure.

### Methods That Separate BC, RL, and Strong Non-RL

- **Author anchor:** exact frozen source/build and derived-weather adapter identity, common complete route, independent replay, both caps.
- **BC-only:** same demonstrations and common initializer used by RL, fixed checkpoint rule, no extra updates.
- **Continued BC:** clone the exact common BC model/optimizer state and spend the same measured training wall/compute as PPO; no confirmation-driven selection.
- **PPO route-RL:** same initialized checkpoint, observation/mask/route action domain and eligible TRAIN maps; freeze reward, failure/terminal treatment, update schedule, checkpoint selection, and all three-or-more independent seeds. If another algorithm is retained, it is a separate corrected contrast, not pooled as extra sample size.
- **Strong same-objective non-RL:** freeze a calibration-selected route-only ALNS with legal multi-trip repacking, plus the best of the approved deterministic strict/annealed controls under the same calibration rule. Include Greedy/insertion/2-opt as diagnostic controls, not as the principal “strongest” comparator. All methods start from the same author anchor and retain the incumbent under both per-map caps.
- Apply the identical 6s and 12s external delivery clocks to all methods. Give each method the same information and the same full-map validator. If shared route candidates/caches are used, charge their creation and access consistently to all methods.

### Endpoints, Statistics, and Failure Rules

- **Primary endpoint:** per-map relative reduction in full-map `M_return` versus the author anchor at each budget, computed only from independently replayed, complete candidates satisfying both caps. Freeze the exact averaging operator and report absolute makespan as well.
- **Co-primary contrasts:** PPO versus (a) frozen BC, (b) wall-clock-matched continued BC, and (c) calibration-selected strongest non-RL, at 6s and 12s. Keep “PPO beats BC” separate from “RL beats strongest non-RL.”
- **Unit/inference:** map is the independent statistical unit; use paired map-level differences and a cluster bootstrap over maps for 95% CIs. Seeds and stochastic solver runs are nested repeats averaged by a frozen rule, never additional map N. Report per-map values and all planned seed outcomes.
- **Failure estimands:** separately report (i) proposal/anchor eligibility over all allocated maps, (ii) complete on-time feasible delivery rate over all eligible maps and all planned seeds, and (iii) conditional quality on eligible maps. For an operational intention-to-treat view, an on-time valid anchor fallback is zero improvement; invalid/late/no-delivery is a delivery failure and cannot disappear from denominators. Do not manufacture an M value for missing routes.
- **Multiplicity:** set familywise alpha 0.05 for the six co-primary PPO contrasts (three comparators × two budgets), using Holm adjustment or a preregistered intersection-union rule requiring every contrast/budget to pass. Label MTC, weather subgroups, emergency outcomes, per-seed analyses, and mechanism diagnostics secondary/exploratory with a declared correction family.
- **Compute and receipts:** freeze CPU/GPU/threads, software/build hashes, wall and core-hour caps for map generation, author solves, training, calibration, and per-map delivery. Persist start/deadline/terminal process receipts, raw candidate, fallback, scorer replay, output hash, and all failures/skips. Train/calibration cost is disclosed separately and cumulatively; no free preprocessing or model load.

## Success and Stop Thresholds

**Scientific success for a constrained RL-utility claim:** on the fresh powered confirmation set, both caps pass per map; 100% of submitted scored routes pass independent complete-route validation; the predeclared operational delivery threshold is met with all planned seeds retained; at both 6s and 12s the map-level mean relative M_return gain meets the approved practical threshold (currently 5%, after reconciling the 0.3% stale plan text), the multiplicity-adjusted 95% lower bound is above zero, and PPO beats frozen BC, continued BC, and the strongest same-objective non-RL under the registered contrasts. Mechanism novelty additionally requires G0 and a successful G6 ablation; empirical performance alone cannot satisfy it.

**Stop/no-claim:** stop before training if G0 fails for a novel-method track, the eligible-map quota/power target cannot be met without replacement, or source/anchor/replay integrity fails. Stop the training package on any predeclared BC feasibility/route-delivery gate failure; preserve all three-seed and per-map failures. After Formal, a CI crossing zero, failure to meet the practical effect threshold, no improvement over continued BC, or parity/loss against the strongest non-RL means no RL-superiority claim. Do not change thresholds, add maps, choose a favorable seed, or reopen P3 afterward. A null result can still support a carefully bounded negative/application-control report if the protocol and package are complete.

## CCF-B Assessment

**Current status: not ready.** The negative P3 result is informative about a specific imitation/free-construction failure mode; the longer teacher-state fit shows only training-set label fit. Neither identifies route improvement or RL contribution. Four-map non-RL calibration and synthetic engineering tests likewise do not establish statistical generalization. The current state has no G0 mechanism, no completed candidate, no PPO-vs-BC result, no strongest-NONRL win, no fresh Formal, no G6 ablation, and no clean G8 package. A CCF-B venue outcome cannot be guaranteed by a numeric gate; at present even the minimum empirical claim required for a route-RL paper is unsupported. The defensible next move is a newly registered, powered, fresh-identity study after G0/claim-class resolution, not a P3 rerun or teacher-fit extension.
