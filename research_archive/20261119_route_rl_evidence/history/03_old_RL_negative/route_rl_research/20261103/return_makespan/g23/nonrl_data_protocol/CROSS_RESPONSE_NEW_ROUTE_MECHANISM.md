# Independent cross-review: threshold-conditioned physical-route policy

Date: 2026-11-03 (Asia/Shanghai)

Reviewed frozen initial: `agents/new_route_mechanism/INITIAL.md`, SHA256 `dcd2e58a0d0dec176e0e31a14dc2222d3b098de1f1df547f5379738e0e147664`.

## Decision

I agree with the frozen initial's **NO-GO for a standalone algorithmic novelty claim**, but not with treating the proposal as an already admissible G0 candidate. Threshold conditioning is a testable route-RL design choice; the current written mechanism is goal/threshold-conditioned sequential route construction with a terminal feasibility indicator, plus a fixed threshold schedule and best timely candidate selection. No problem-specific mathematical or algorithmic increment beyond those components is specified.

## Point-by-point objections and required distinctions

1. **The metric is already present in Liu.** Liu's model objective is total cost (full text, `audit/papers/Liu2026.txt`, lines 234–236), but §5.3 reports both total cost and latest task completion time (lines 756–757). The official implementation computes `latestTime` as `max_d sum_trip calculatePathTime(trip)` in `reference/java/official_source/Check.java:594–617`; `calculatePathTime` includes travel and target service (`:22–47`). The new scorer's `M_return=max_slot sum_trip(travel+service)` is the same operational quantity under this model's zero battery-swap delay and all-vehicles-start-at-zero assumptions (`return_makespan/scorer.py:162–210`). Thus this is a **change of primary optimization emphasis**, not a newly introduced metric or a metric Liu ignored.

2. **The author already has a load-balancing route repair.** Liu Eq. (23), §4.3.2 evaluates feasible insertions across all drone routes using drone workload and system-cost terms (`audit/papers/Liu2026.txt:545–561`). This does not prove that Liu solves an explicit global min-max problem; `T_max` in Eq. (23) is a duty limit, not current global makespan. It does mean the author-faithful comparator must retain this operator. A result that beats only a cost-only output while omitting load-balance insertion would not isolate the value of RL.

3. **Min-max route RL and preference conditioning are established ingredients.** Li et al. explicitly cover min-sum/min-max heterogeneous CVRP and sequential vehicle/node route construction (source and scope summarized in `project_status/20261103/metric_replan/PRIMARY_METRIC_EVIDENCE.md`). PSL provides preference-conditioned multiobjective combinatorial policies; E-AM reports route balancing, vehicle-customer pairs and preference-conditioned DRL, with exact balance equation still UNKNOWN pending full text. These do not prove equivalence to LRPHSD's cumulative multi-trip battery/duty problem, but they prevent claiming threshold conditioning, preference input, heterogeneous vehicle-target decisions, or min-max RL as the mechanism novelty by themselves.

4. **The non-RL threshold control can exploit the same structure.** With deployment, target-station ownership, and all vehicle starts fixed, each station's route construction is separable except for the single full-map cost cap; the global makespan is the maximum station/slot cumulative duty. For a proposed threshold `tau`, a non-RL feasibility/repair method can seek station routes with every slot duty at most `tau`, then check the summed full-map cost against the same anchor. A threshold search over `tau`, stationwise min-max route packing, and joint min-max ALNS therefore form mandatory controls. The old station split DP minimized additive total route time and cannot be relabeled a min-max reference; it needs a separately frozen objective and code path if adapted.

5. **The terminal reward is sparse and may not carry the claimed learning signal.** `1{feasible, C<=C0, M<=tau}` provides no ordering among successful routes or gradients between an almost-successful and a poor failed route. Any shaping, critic target, or replay rule must be specified before training and compared to the same direct-route actor without `tau`. Selecting several `tau` values is extra rollouts and output search, all inside the 6s/12s budget; no threshold may be selected after seeing a test-map result.

6. **The positive prediction is not a novelty proof.** Beating Liu's original cost objective on `M_return` would be an application result only. To support an RL contribution, the policy must also beat (a) Liu's faithful route component/load-balance mode evaluated on `M_return`, (b) a strong same-objective non-RL min-max method, and (c) an unconditioned direct-route RL control. Every method needs the same complete feasible anchor, state/information, validator, fixed target/slot scope, time limits, and failure/fallback accounting. End-to-end claims require a separate frozen E comparison; the approved D conditional route result does not establish full TSALNS superiority.

## Independent data and protocol findings

For an axis-aligned square with arbitrary translation, the combined target+site `x/y` spans fit the paper-stated side length for **65/120**: by candidate-site count `I=3: 24/30`, `I=4: 19/30`, `I=5: 2/10`, `I=6: 0/10`, `I=7: 0/10`, `I=8: 0/10`, `I=9: 10/10`, `I=10: 10/10`. The previously reported 36/120 is the stricter literal `[0,L]` coordinate-box count and must not be called a mismatch by itself because the paper does not specify the origin. The 65/120 translated-extent result is stronger: 55 maps cannot fit an axis-aligned square of the stated side under any translation. Using a 15,000 m side for `I<=8` makes all 120 extents fit their respective side lengths, but fitting extents does not prove that this was the generator.
- The paper states uniform generation plus 200 m target spacing, >2,000 m candidate-site spacing, and nearest-site distance no greater than DABS radius (`audit/papers/Liu2026.txt:580–584`). The included official source/data tree contains pre-generated instances and the solver's file reader, but no source generator or generation seed. Therefore an exact source-faithful generator is not evidenced; new maps must be labeled **constraint-reconstructed from the paper**, with every distribution assumption stated, unless the missing author generator is obtained. Do not silently replace 14,000 with 15,000 after seeing quality outcomes.
- All 120 official map inputs have prior reproduction/metric exposure, and the old Stage-A 24-map validation subset is included in them. Freeze a manifest of all prior input hashes; new training/development/validation/Formal inputs need new identities. Check raw SHA256 and canonical coordinate/task-attribute identities so renaming or translation does not convert an exposed map into a new map. Formal maps must be generated only after methods, seeds, budgets, and statistics are frozen.

## Recommended frozen G2/G3 protocol (subject to root approval)

### Objective and comparator matrix

- Keep the approved D estimand: same per-map anchor `R0`, same complete original evaluator and hard constraints, same fixed deployment/type/target-station set, and full cost cap `C<=C0` with epsilon 0/tolerance `1e-7`. Report raw `C-C0`, fixed and operating costs, and `M_return`; do not add a post-hoc percentage tolerance.
- D candidate output must be on-time and independently valid. Anchor fallback remains the scored `M0` with explicit method-failure/fallback flags; no on-time validated output is a failed cell, not a dropped map. Include model loading, policy calls, all threshold queries, construction, validation, retries, and fallback in the D clock.
- Freeze distinct comparator identities: no-op common author-derived anchor; author-faithful route-component/cost mode including Eq. (23) loadBalanceInsertion; cheap balanced-insertion control; same-objective non-RL joint min-max route/slot/trip-repacking method under the same cap; an exact/DP min-max reference only on its prespecified solvable subdomain; unconditioned direct-route RL; threshold-conditioned direct-route RL. Do not call a fixed-domain author route adaptation the full original TSALNS.
- If claiming end-to-end superiority to full Liu TSALNS, run a separate E comparison from raw map to final output under the same total time and identical external initialization. Report the author's faithful output's cost and `M_return`; D alone supports only a conditional route-improvement claim.

### Maps and allocation

- Freeze 48 new exploratory maps as 4 per each of the 12 published `(candidate sites, targets)` families; freeze up to 24 separate non-RL calibration/validation maps before results (a stratified split such as 12 calibration + 12 validation is a proposal, not a current state fact). Keep all family/feasibility failures in the denominator.
- Until the generator mismatch is resolved, describe the generator as constraint-reconstructed; publish selected side lengths, independent sampling protocol, spacing/rejection rules, service/energy values, station/target counts, coverage acceptance, seed list, and generator hash. Use one protocol across all methods. Preserve separate seeds for map generation versus solver/RL randomness.
- Stay within the approved first-package cap: at most 48 opportunity/development maps, at most 24 separate non-RL calibration/validation maps, and 16 CPU core-hours total for all new non-RL generation, calibration, candidate generation, validation, failures and analysis. Record CPU wall time and per-process thread count. G4 GPU/CPU training resources remain a separate package.

### Statistics and claim rules

- Map is the paired statistical unit; aggregate RL training seeds by a rule fixed before comparisons and separately report per-seed spread. Compare each method at both 6s and 12s; include every map and failure. Keep the existing mean-reduction, CI, win/loss, feasibility and cost-cap gates; control the frozen comparison family rather than selecting a baseline after results.
- The threshold-conditioned policy is admissible as a G4 empirical treatment only if a substantive mechanism is independently approved at G0. Its success criterion is both the frozen quality threshold and superiority over the strongest same-objective non-RL method; superiority over Liu's cost-only output is not sufficient evidence that RL itself helped.
- No result is presently available for new-map quality, non-RL M improvement, or RL. The current report does not approve threshold-conditioned RL for training.
