## The finite-space identity and its precise limited implication

Let `x=Phi(tau)` be the unordered slot-tagged **ordered** nonempty trip collection, and `q_theta(x)=sum_{Phi(tau)=x} p_theta(tau)`. With a theta-independent partition, differentiable common support and finite sums:

```text
grad log q_theta(x)
  = sum_{Phi(tau)=x} [p_theta(tau)/q_theta(x)] grad log p_theta(tau).
E[(R(x)-b(I)) grad log p_theta(tau) | x,I]
  = (R(x)-b(I)) grad log q_theta(x).
```

The second equality needs class-constant terminal reward and an instance-only detached baseline. The law of total covariance yields the per-draw fixed-theta covariance reduction. Uniform averaging of trajectory scores gives another quantity unless the within-class trajectory probabilities happen to be equal. `E_uniform[log p]`, `log sum p`, a canonical representative's log probability, and SRT's imitation objective must remain distinct.

This calculation is my application of the existing marginal-score identity. It is not a novelty proof. The `O(K 2^K)` recursion is exact only when boundary-state and every within-block actor probability are recoverable from subset `A` plus the fixed block prefix. Recurrent/history-dependent actor states, path-sensitive masks and stochastic layers require extra conditioning or invalidate that compression. Canonical numeric aggregation must also preserve the actual sampled likelihood, rather than silently defining another policy for replay.

An invariant event such as `K<=cap` can select exact conditionalization with original-score fallback on its complement. An ordering-dependent truncation does not inherit that proof. A more refined bounded partition may be possible, but its cells must be stated before claiming unbiasedness. I have not read the separate window proposal and make no judgment about its actual construction here.
