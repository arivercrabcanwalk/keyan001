## Small mathematical clarifications to the object

The initial's open-trip minutes and energy include the already flown prefix. An implementation evaluating future cost-to-go must subtract the already incurred operating cost or keep it as an explicit constant; otherwise it double counts prefix cost in the reward. The residual duty row correctly includes it once. Both conventions rank completions identically at a fixed state, but only a consistent convention gives the correct action-level Bellman difference.

A fixed-endpoint/subset shortest route weakly dominates longer routes because flight minutes, flight energy and operating cost are monotone in the same length. This is not a claim that subset selection or partial-prefix decisions are trivial. An updated open endpoint changes the relevant shortest-path start, so stale depot-tour columns cannot simply be reused as exact continuation costs after a visit. Removing a visited target can preserve some subset inclusions, but it does not preserve sequence weights or remaining-prefix resource budgets.

The initial's O(n^2*2^n) shortest-tour and O(3^n) partition estimates are conventional single-pool recurrence costs. A full prefix-conditioned implementation must account separately for each currently open endpoint, slot tags, eligible continuation/fresh families, storage, reconstruction and any pool refresh. These equations do not advertise a new polynomial-time update, and the station-G2 cutoff is not automatically a valid online policy cost budget.
