# Joint Route RL / RSCC Prior-Art and Gradient Audit

Date: 2026-11-03. Scope: fixed stations, station modes and station-owned target sets; direct physical `slot,target` / `slot,RETURN` decisions only. No solver, evaluator, training or quality run was performed.

## Decision

The physical action domain is valid under the user's route-only boundary, but its generic decision grammar and its counterfactual-credit ingredient are not a new mechanism. Heterogeneous vehicle-node route construction, refueling/return actions, pairwise vehicle-customer values, centralized allocation, centralized critics and counterfactual action baselines all have primary-source precedents. The RSCC original flat-pair formula also has a policy-gradient defect. The mechanism agent corrected that defect in `G4_CANDIDATE_REV1.md`; the corrected factorized formula is mathematically coherent as a standard conditional actor-critic credit decomposition, but no non-direct algorithm increment over those precedents has been identified. **G0 remains NO-GO; do not start RSCC training on the strength of this design.**

The fixed-station DABS problem still has a real discrete choice over the LD/SD partition of station-owned targets. However, with additive per-slot operating cost, per-sortie energy and cumulative-duty constraints local to each slot, and no synchronization/shared charging resource, interleaving slot actions is only a construction order. Every completed joint sequence corresponds to a disjoint target partition plus independent closed multi-trip slot routes, and the reverse construction is feasible by interleaving those routes. The station split-DP / assignment-and-route method therefore remains a mandatory strong non-RL comparator. This structural observation is a model decomposition, not a performance result.

## RSCC Formula Audit

### Frozen flat-pair formula: reject

The original candidate defines a single categorical action `(d,i)` and

`b(s,i) = sum_d pi(d|s,i) Q(s,(d,i))`,
`A(s,(d,i)) = -Q(s,(d,i)) + b(s,i)`.

For the joint pair policy, this baseline depends on `i`, which is part of the sampled joint action. A score-function baseline must be independent of the action whose score it multiplies. Under the autoregressive factorization `pi(i|s) pi(d|s,i)`, and exact Q/weights, `E_d[A | s,i] = 0`; applying A to the **joint** log-probability therefore removes the target/event head's conditional credit (and with an approximate Q generally biases it). The frozen flat-pair formula is not an admissible central-policy advantage as written.

### Revised factorized formula: mathematically coherent, not novel

The mechanism agent's `G4_CANDIDATE_REV1.md` repairs this by selecting a physical event `u` (visit target or a return event), then a feasible slot `d`, with `pi_U(u|s) pi_D(d|s,u)`. For reward-valued `Q(s,u,d)`:

`Qbar_U(s,u) = sum_d pi_D(d|s,u) Q(s,u,d)`
`b_U(s) = sum_u pi_U(u|s) Qbar_U(s,u)`
`A_U(s,u) = Qbar_U(s,u) - b_U(s)`
`A_D(s,u,d) = Q(s,u,d) - Qbar_U(s,u)`.

`b_U` is state-only for the event score; `Qbar_U` is a valid conditional baseline for the slot score only. Summing the two separate score-function terms recovers the ordinary joint-policy gradient when the critic equals the on-policy action value and the baselines are detached from the actor objective. The event term remains `Qbar_U-b_U`, so the target/event credit is not algebraically canceled. With shared actor parameters, the two log-probability score terms still need explicit separate weighting. If PPO is used, its ratio/clipping convention for each factor must be specified and tested; the exact policy-gradient identity alone does not establish equivalence to a particular clipped PPO objective.

This is a standard autoregressive vehicle/node policy with a state baseline on the first factor and a same-event conditional counterfactual baseline on the second. COMA and related conditional-baseline methods are the nearest algorithmic prior art; the problem-specific feasible-slot set does not make the baseline itself new.

## Specific Challenges / Remaining Gaps

1. **No novelty beyond adaptation:** give an exact algorithmic increment beyond Li's vehicle/node policy, COMA's conditional action marginalization, Hoppe's counterfactual vehicle-request credit, and pairwise value-guided assignment. “The slot is SD/LD and duty-constrained” names LRPHSD's problem attributes, not yet a new algorithm.
2. **Continuation-Q identification:** on-policy terminal returns label only sampled actions. State how every unchosen feasible `(u,d)` gets a Q target: branched on-policy continuation rollouts, TD/generalized critic learning, or another method. Report bias/variance and all counterfactual-query costs. If branches are simulated, share comparable information/compute with non-RL controls.
3. **Completion risk is not yet modeled:** RSCC_REV1's Q is expected final operating reward/cost under a continuation policy, not an explicit probability or certificate of route completion. Specify dead-end/infeasible-terminal treatment; a one-step legal mask does not certify that the remaining targets can be completed. Compare any learned completion-risk feature against exact/bounded station split-DP feasibility.
4. **Correct training objective:** detach critic values and probability-weighted baselines in the actor loss. For PPO, freeze whether the policy ratio is joint or separately factorized, whether the two factor advantages are clipped separately, and how value targets are normalized. Unit-test against exact expected gradients in a finite toy MDP before quality training; this is correctness validation, not novelty evidence.
5. **No shared physical resource:** prove any claimed slot interaction is not merely the target partition already represented by station-level assignment-and-route DP. The absent synchronization/makespan/charging constraints cannot be silently added to create coupling.
6. **Comparators:** at minimum compare standard factorized PPO on the same `(event,slot)` physical action domain, Li-style vehicle-node policy, pairwise vehicle-target value allocation, strong station partition-and-route methods, bounded DP/beam and station ALNS/SISR. Match training/inference and rollout compute, and report full-map original objective at the frozen 6s/12s gates.
