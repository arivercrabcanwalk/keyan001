# Transfer Plan

This export preserves the project's actual G0-G8 contract while making the materials readable to a new assistant.

1. **G0 mechanism and prior-art audit**: define a concrete physical route mechanism, its full input/output, closest prior art, and falsifiable prediction. A generic pointer policy, mask, RETURN action, larger network, or a renamed operator selector is not a mechanism.
2. **G1 correctness**: verify target/RETURN actions, energy/time feasibility, cumulative duty, exactly-once service, independent replay, failure accounting, and rollback.
3. **G2 opportunity diagnosis**: use a fresh, frozen development population; separate conditional route opportunity from whole-system claims.
4. **G3 non-RL qualification**: compare the strongest same-information rule, greedy, beam, enumeration where bounded, supervised/bandit controls, and the author anchor with equal candidate-generation and wall-clock accounting.
5. **G4 route RL**: only if G3 exposes a real opportunity, train at least three independent seeds. The policy may output only physical route actions, never search-operator choices.
6. **G5 formal evaluation**: freeze code, model, data, budget and statistics, then use a new independent formal population.
7. **G6 ablation/audit**: isolate each proposed innovation and report map-level effects, uncertainty and all failures.
8. **G7/G8 paper package**: only after the evidence gates pass; otherwise publish a transparent negative/engineering report rather than an unsupported superiority claim.

The present evidence stops before a positive G4 result. The next assistant should audit and extend only under this contract.
